# 🤖 PsycedelicAI Custom GPT Archive

Welcome to the **Custom GPT and chatbot archive** of **PsycedelicAI**.

This folder contains old prompts, Custom GPT experiments, chatbot concepts, AI agency ideas, coaching-platform notes, creative assistants, research tools and other experiments created while exploring what specialised AI systems could become.

Most of the material in this folder is more than **two years old**.

That makes this collection more than a set of prompts. It is also a record of an earlier stage in the development of AI tools, prompt engineering and human-AI collaboration.

Some of the work is practical.

Some is experimental.

Some is unfinished.

Some started as an idea in Notion and later became a GPT, chatbot, platform concept or reusable prompt.

This is an archive of that process.

> **A prompt can become a tool.  
> A tool can become a chatbot.  
> A chatbot can become a system.**

---

## 📜 Why this archive exists

AI changes quickly.

A prompt that was useful two years ago may work differently with modern AI models. Some instructions may now be unnecessary. Other ideas may still be useful, but require adaptation to current platforms and capabilities.

The purpose of this archive is to preserve:

- old prompt designs
- early AI concepts
- experimental chatbot structures
- ideas that later developed into larger projects
- examples of how specialised AI assistants were designed
- the evolution of PsycedelicAI thinking over time

The files should therefore be viewed as **historical, experimental and creative material**.

They are not all finished products.

They are not all actively maintained.

Some may be obsolete.

Some may still contain useful ideas.

# 🔄 Using old prompts with modern AI

Older prompts can be tested with current AI models, but they may need adaptation.

When reviewing an old prompt, consider:

- Which parts are still useful?
- Which instructions are outdated?
- Was the prompt compensating for limitations in an older model?
- Can the prompt be made shorter?
- Should it be divided into separate modules?
- Does it need updated tools or knowledge files?
- What original idea is still worth preserving?

The goal is not always to use the prompt exactly as it is.

Sometimes the most valuable part is the idea behind it.
