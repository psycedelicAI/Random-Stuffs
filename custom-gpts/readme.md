# 🚀 Chatbot Projects

## 🤖 AI Chatbot | Single Agent

A chatbot concept based around a single AI agent.

The idea is to create one focused AI assistant that can be configured for a specific purpose, personality, workflow, or service.

Possible uses include:

- customer support
- personal assistance
- specialised guidance
- lead handling
- information support
- interactive business workflows

The original project material includes screenshots and configuration notes for the chatbot setup.

---

## 🏢 AI Chatbot | Agency

A broader chatbot-agency concept for creating and delivering AI chatbot solutions for businesses and projects.

The agency model explores how multiple chatbot services could be offered through a structured platform, including:

- chatbot creation
- specialised AI agents
- customer support systems
- lead-generation tools
- business integrations
- client workspaces
- AI coaching products
- commercial deployment options

The project also includes notes about sales, Fiverr advertising, Stripe payments, and the wider service model.

This material represents an evolving project concept. It should not automatically be interpreted as a fully deployed production platform.

## 🧑‍🏫 AI Coach Platform

A platform concept for creating customised AI coaches and delivering them to clients, communities, or businesses.

### Customisation options

- Customise personality
- Customise coaching style
- Customise avatar appearance
- Create a coach from scratch

### AI training and development

- Train an AI with links, PDFs, and text
- Lead-generation technology options
- Customer-support technology options
- Coaching technology options

### Product management and integration

- Sell multiple products per AI coach
- Create unlimited workspaces
- Create unlimited AI coaches
- Capture unlimited leads
- Embed unlimited AI coaches
- Integrate AI coaches into applications
- Offer additional premium AI coaches

### Client interaction and CRM

- Let AI coaches collect reviews
- Let AI coaches collect feedback
- Connect client CRM technology
- Support client-facing coaching workflows

### Educational and support resources

- Training and tutorials
- Knowledgebase access
- Online embedding
- Client guidance and onboarding

### Commercial rights

The concept includes the possibility of offering commercial rights to businesses.
