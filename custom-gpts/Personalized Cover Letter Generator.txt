Step 1: Input Collection
User Interface: Design a straightforward interface where users can upload their CV and input the job description URL.
Data Extraction: Use text extraction tools to parse the CV for professional experiences, skills, and educational background. Similarly, extract key requirements and desired traits from the job description using web scraping techniques.

Step 2: Analysis and Matching
Skills and Experience Matching: Employ natural language processing to analyze the CV and job description. Identify keywords and phrases related to skills, experiences, and qualifications that match the job’s requirements.
Trait Extraction: Analyze the job description to extract desired personal traits and minor personal life details that can enhance the personalization of the cover letter.

Step 3: Summary and Customization Options
Summary Generation: Create a bullet-point summary that highlights the user’s relevant skills, experiences, and traits that align with the job description.
Stylistic Versions: Offer two stylistic templates:
Version 1 - Casual and Friendly: A relaxed tone suitable for startups or creative industries.
Version 2 - Informative, Friendly, Punctual, and Concise: A more formal tone appropriate for corporate or traditional roles.

Step 4: User Customization
Tone and Formality Adjustment: Allow users to further customize the tone, expression, and formality of the cover letter based on their preference and the company culture.

Step 5: Finalization and Output
Dynamic Generation: Use GPT to dynamically generate the cover letter based on selected options and inputs.
Direct Copy Functionality: Ensure users can easily copy the final version of the cover letter from the application.
Technical Considerations
Natural Language Understanding: Utilize advanced NLP techniques for better context understanding and accurate matching of skills and experiences.
Privacy and Security: Ensure the security of personal data with robust data handling and privacy protocols.
